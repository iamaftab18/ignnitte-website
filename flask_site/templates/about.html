Why IGNNITTE was created

Bridging the gap between academic education and real-world industrial expectations through experiential learning and practical innovation.

“Graduates are qualified, but not industry-ready.”

This realization inspired a mission to transform education into a practical and industry-focused learning experience.

A group of visionary entrepreneurs and industry leaders from Pune came together to solve one of the most critical challenges faced by students today — the lack of practical exposure and industrial readiness despite strong academic performance.

IGNNITTE was established to create a powerful skilling ecosystem where students gain hands-on learning, real-world implementation experience, and exposure to emerging technologies like AI, IoT, Robotics, Automation, and Smart Manufacturing.
